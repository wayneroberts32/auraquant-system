🔎 What those things are called (TradingView vocabulary)
A) Global Chrome

Symbol Tabs / Chart Tabs (top left): one tab per symbol/layout. Shows ticker, last price, %. × closes, + adds.

Global Search (top center): find symbols, people, ideas.

Global Actions (top right): layout manager, screen/fullscreen, app menu.

B) Dashboard “Widgets / Feature Panels”

Cards on the home/dashboard that act like big buttons with previews:

Supercharts – open advanced chart workspace; “Create new layout”.

Screeners – jump into stock/ETF/crypto/FX screeners; opens a modal first, then the screener.

Calendars – economic & earnings calendars; preview of events; opens full calendar view.

News Flow – real-time headlines feed with right-side article reader.

Heatmaps – market treemap (S&P, Crypto, etc.), color by change %, size by cap.

Options – payoff diagram builder for strategies.

Markets – world indices overview.

Community – social/ideas (in AuraQuant: link to Telegram/Discord).

C) Advanced Chart Workspace (Supercharts)

Top Toolbar

Symbol Search – change instrument, add to chart.

Resolution/Interval Picker – 1m, 5m, 15m, 1H, 1D, 1W, 1M, custom.

Chart Type – candles, bars, line, area, Heikin Ashi.

Compare/Add Symbol – overlay multiple symbols.

Indicators & Strategies – opens library; add/remove studies.

Templates – save/reapply indicator/visual templates.

Alerts – create/edit price & indicator alerts.

Replay / Bar Replay – go back in time and play bars forward.

Layout Split – 1×1, 2×1, 2×2, 3×1, etc. sync symbol/timeframe optional.

Screenshot/Share/Publish Idea – export or publish snapshot.

Settings – chart settings (scales, trading, appearance).

Left Drawing Toolbar

Cursor/Crosshair; Trend line/Ray/Extended; Info line; Horizontal/Vertical/Trend lines; Parallel channel; Fib tools; Brush; Text/Notes; Patterns (Elliott, Head & Shoulders); Long/Short position tool (risk boxes); Icons; Measure; Zoom; Magnet; Lock; Hide drawings; Remove drawings.

Right Sidebar

Watchlist; Alerts Manager; Details / Data Window; Hotlists; Calendar/News; My Ideas; Public/Private Chats; Streams; Help.

Bottom Panel (Terminal)

Trading Panel – broker connections/order entry (panel lists brokers).

Pine Editor – code editor for indicators/strategies.

Strategy Tester – run backtests on the loaded strategy and report metrics.

Chart Overlays & Scales

OHLC & last price labels (green/red), watermark, last trade line, session breaks, extended session shading, separate panes for indicators, price scale modes (regular / log / percent), auto/fit buttons, time scale zoom & drag.

D) Screener (Stocks/ETFs/FX/Crypto)

Preset & Saved screens, Filters (market, exchange, sector, fundamentals: P/E, Beta, EPS growth, div yield; technicals: RSI, ATR, gaps; events: earnings), Columns, Sorting, Save/Share/Export, Alerts on screens.

E) Economic & Earnings Calendar

Tabs: Economic, Earnings, Revenue, Dividends.

Filters: Country/Region, Importance, Category.

Day/week grid with Actual / Forecast / Prior values; subscribe/alerts.

F) News Flow

Filters: Watchlist, Symbol, Market, Crypto, Economics, Provider, Priority, Sentiment.

Left list + right article reader (share buttons, tags, provider).

G) Heatmap

Universe selector (e.g., S&P 500), Group by Sector/Industry, Color by % change (or other metric), Size by market cap, search, zoom, fullscreen/capture.

H) Options (strategy builder)

Select underlying, expiry, strikes, build call/put legs; show payoff curve with max profit/loss, breakevens; Greeks/implied vol sliders in advanced views.
